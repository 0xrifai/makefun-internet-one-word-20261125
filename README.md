# The Internet in One Word

One single word describing the whole internet.

## The word

**Groupchat**

## Why this word

The internet is one giant group chat: billions of people typing at once,
nobody reading, someone always replying to a message from three days ago,
and the notifications never, ever stop. It is not one app, one site, or one
mood — it is everybody in the same room talking over each other, forever.
Someone is always typing, someone is always leaving, someone always comes
back with "anyway" and starts a whole new argument at 3 a.m.

## How the word was picked

Nine candidates were weighed in-session before settling on one: Unhinged,
Receipts, Feral, Unverified, Scrollhole, Echochamber, Everyone, Loudest,
and Groupchat. Unhinged caught the vibe but only the mood, not the whole
machine. Receipts was funny — nothing online ever really disappears — but
it leans on slang to land. Feral was punchy and short, yet it describes a
tone rather than the thing itself. Unverified was clever and true, but too
dry to make anyone laugh out loud. Scrollhole and Echochamber each caught
only one habit of the internet, and Everyone and Loudest were too flat to
be funny on their own. Groupchat won because it pictures the entire
internet in one beat: every person on earth, one room, all talking at
once, no one listening — funny by itself, true of the whole thing, and not
the first obvious word anyone reaches for.

## How it works

`main.ts` writes the word to the absolute path `/output/entry.txt` — no
trailing newline, no explanation, just the word itself.

```sh
npx tsx main.ts
```
